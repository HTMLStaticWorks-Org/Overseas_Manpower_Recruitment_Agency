9-Stage Deployment Roadmap

A Clear & Trusted Path to Your Overseas Career.

From initial registration to international arrival, every milestone follows strict compliance and transparent communication.

Step-by-Step Timeline

Complete Recruitment Journey

Nine structured milestones designed to protect candidates and deliver vetted talent to international employers.

STAGE 01

Candidate Registration

Create your GlobeHire account with verified contact details and preferred destination.

STAGE 02

Resume Submission

Upload your updated CV, trade certificates, and passport validity information.

STAGE 03

Profile Evaluation

Sector specialists verify experience, licensing eligibility, and language readiness.

STAGE 04

Employer Shortlisting

Matched profiles are presented directly to hiring managers at partner companies.

STAGE 05

Interview & Selection

Attend structured video, panel, or practical trade-test assessments.

STAGE 06

Job Offer Confirmation

Review and sign your formal employment contract outlining salary and benefits.

STAGE 07

Medical & Documentation

Complete authorized medical fitness exams, police clearance, and attestations.

STAGE 08

Visa Processing

Employer-sponsored work permit and embassy visa stamping coordination.

STAGE 09

Travel & Deployment

Pre-departure briefing, flight ticketing, and airport-to-accommodation handover.

Compliance Checklist

Documentation & Visa Assistance

Our compliance desk guides you through every required credential so embassy and ministry filings proceed smoothly.

DOC 1

Passport Requirements

Machine-readable passport valid for at least 12–18 months with blank visa pages.

DOC 2

Educational Certificates

Degree, diploma, or trade certifications with embassy/apostille attestation guidance.

DOC 3

Experience Certificates

Verified employment reference letters detailing role tenure and responsibilities.

DOC 4

Medical Documentation

GAMCA / destination-approved clinic medical fitness reports and vaccination records.

DOC 5

Work Permit & Visa Guidance

Coordinated biometrics, visa application tracking, and employment entry permits.

Readiness & Onboarding

Candidate Support & Preparation

We prepare every selected professional for both the technical interview and life in their new destination country.

Interview Preparation

Mock technical briefings, role-specific expectations, and employer culture insights.

Pre-Departure Guidance

Orientation on local labor rights, banking setup, SIM cards, and housing.

Travel Preparation

Flight itinerary coordination, baggage rules, and airport pickup contacts.

Country-Specific Guidance

Tailored briefings for GCC, EU, and APAC workplace standards and etiquette.
